## Contract shape

When you shape a contract the change sets or changes:

- Frame the problem first: what any shape must satisfy, and what it depends on.
- Judge a shape by what its callers see: its interface, its invariants and error modes, and one caller's use of it. Also
  judge what it hides behind the seam, and where its leverage is high or thin.
- Name things in the codebase's own words, for the modules, seams and domain terms it already has.
