## Plain English

Write prose the reader takes in on one pass, most of the way to ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English: keep its
writing rules, not its controlled dictionary.

- One idea per sentence: at most 20 words for an instruction, 25 for a description, 30 for a requirement.
- One topic per paragraph, in at most six sentences.
- Active voice and simple tenses: "the tick posts the spec", not "the spec is being posted".
- One term for one thing, the term `GLOSSARY.md` uses, and one meaning per term.
- Keep the articles and the small words; do not drop them to save space.
- No stack of more than three nouns: "the findings of the check", not "spec check finding list".
- Plain verbs, not phrasal verbs or verbs made into nouns: "decide", not "make a decision on".
- Code, paths, flags and identifiers stay as written, in backticks.
