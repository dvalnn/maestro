Standards are CODING_STANDARDS.md and the `area` attachment, when a preset attaches one; the spec is the request, its
requirement IDs and the ADRs under docs/adr/. Every finding cites in its issue the rule, requirement or ADR it breaks,
names its file:line in where, and states the failure. Drop any finding you cannot cite.

An acceptance item the change leaves unmet blocks. An item no change could meet as written, such as a command that runs
no test, is the spec's fault and out of review: name it in the report's gaps.

The lane's commit message is a placeholder the tour manager replaces on landing: its subject and closing line are out of
review.
